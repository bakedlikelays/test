Advanced & troubleshooting 

      

      
    

  


  

    
If it won't connect

    
    
      
  • Close any other app that might be connected to the light, and turn the light's power off and on. These controllers only accept one connection at a time.
    • 
      
  • Turn on Bluetooth (and on Android, Location) on your phone. Chrome asks for permission the first time.
    • 
      
  • Lights usually show up with names like ELK-BLEDOM, MELK, LEDBLE, Triones, QHM- or BJ_LED. If yours has a different name, tick "Show every nearby Bluetooth device" under Advanced.
    • 
      
  • If it connects but nothing changes, open Advanced and try the other protocol options one at a time.
    • 
      
  • If the light only came with a small infrared remote and never had a Bluetooth app, a phone can't control it this way.
    •
